About Klookup

Klookup is an experimental visual clock
made of 1,440 illustrated minutes.

Every minute is a small visual moment,
part clock, part promotional banner,
and an invitation to discover an artist’s portfolio.

Some minutes are created by artists.
Others are AI-generated and inspired by selected artworks.

Want to create a minute, collaborate,
or support the project?
